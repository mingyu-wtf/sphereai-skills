# Naver Blog Strategy

Use this when a beginner asks **how to run a Naver blog**: what categories to make, how often to post, how posts link to each other, what to check each week. It only produces a plan; it does not touch the blog.

## Six basic principles
1. **One reader, one topic area.** Say in one line who the blog is for. Start with 2-3 categories.
2. **Hub and detail posts.** For each theme, write one hub post that lists the detailed posts, and link each detailed post back to the hub.
3. **Link your own posts.** At the end of a post, link to your own posts that are already public.
4. **A rhythm you can keep.** Pick a posting schedule you can hold for 3 months, and schedule posts ahead.
5. **Be honest.** Name your sources, hedge uncertain facts, no fake experience, no invented numbers or links.
6. **Look at the numbers once a week** and adjust the plan.

## Six steps
1. **Ask 4 questions**: who is the reader, what do you know or have material for, how many posts a week can you keep, and whether the blog will have paid or affiliate links (if yes, say so at the top of each post).
2. **Name the blog and 2-3 categories**, one line of purpose for each.
3. **List 10 first posts** with `naver-blog-topic-picker`: one hub post and the detailed posts around it.
4. **Set the schedule**: which days and hours.
5. **Add the link plan**: for each post, which of your public posts it links to and which hub it belongs to.
6. **Make a weekly checklist**: open the statistics (see `naver-blog-measurement`), note the top posts and search words, and write one sentence on what to write next.

## Output
A one-page plan: blog one-liner, categories, the 10-post list with dates, the schedule, the link plan and the weekly checklist. Ask the user to approve it before any writing starts.

## What not to promise
No ranking, no home-feed placement, no income. Naver decides these and they change.
