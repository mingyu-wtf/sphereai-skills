# Photo rules

- Keep the user's original files untouched; work on copies.
- Rename in visiting order: `01_gate.jpg`, `02_sign.jpg`, `03_inside.jpg` ... The order of the post follows the order of the visit.
- Resize to 900-1080 px wide for the editor. Crop to 3:2 or 4:3 landscape where it helps; do not stretch.
- Remove: blurry shots, near-duplicates (keep the best one), photos where the subject is unclear.
- Hide: faces of other people (blur or crop), car plates, receipts, card numbers, phone screens with personal data. If a receipt is the only source of a price, type the price into a table and do not post the receipt image.
- The first photo should show the place clearly (sign or building), because it often becomes the thumbnail.
- 10-15 photos is a good amount for a visit post. If the user sent fewer than 6, say so and ask if there are more before writing.
- Caption every photo with one plain line saying what is in it ("BAR&CAFE 방향 안내 표지").
