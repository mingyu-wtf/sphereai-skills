# Threads Popular Post Research (basic)

Before writing, look at what already gets reactions on your topic. Take the **structure**, never the sentences. This skill is read only: no posting, replying, liking or following.

## Steps (20-30 minutes)
1. **Search** with the browser tool: `https://www.threads.com/search?q=<keyword>&serp_type=default` (top) and `&filter=recent` (latest). Keywords come from `config.json` `keywords.research`.
2. **Read the numbers** (likes, comments, reposts, shares) on each post's own page. If you cannot read a number, write "not read". Never guess.
3. **Note the structure**: first line, length, numbered list or not, how it ends, link or no link, image or not.
4. **Skip** posts built on conflict, adult or gambling topics, and posts that ask for comments, follows or reposts in exchange for something.
5. **Add 1-3 rows** to `research/viral_analysis.md`:

| Date | Keyword | First line (structure only) | Likes | Comments | Shares | Format | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

6. **Post ideas**: list 3-5 topics that fit what you saw and are not already in the queue. Hand them to `threads-info-post-writer`.

## Rules
- Numbers only as read on screen, with the date.
- Do not copy other people's sentences, and do not name their accounts in public posts.

## Output
The rows added, and 3-5 post ideas.
