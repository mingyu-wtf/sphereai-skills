# Vertical Highlight Reshoot

Cropping 16:9 to 9:16 throws away two thirds of the picture and usually cuts the subject. Because the video is 3D, re-render it with vertical cameras instead.

## 1. Pick the highlight (60-90 s)
- Choose whole sentences, in order, that tell a complete mini-story: hook -> one strong consequence -> the twist.
- If a sentence starts with "this" or "these", keep the sentence before it too.
- If it is too long, drop a whole block, not half sentences.
- Write the pick as a list: section, original frame from-to, sentence text.

## 2. Re-shoot
- Back up the original 16:9 files first and keep the original scene scripts untouched.
- Write a small wrapper per section that runs the original scene, then changes resolution to 1080x1920 and moves the camera.
- Side-by-side comparisons become stacked: move one object in front of or above the other.
- Vertical frames show more floor and sky: extend the ground so its edges are not visible.

## 3. Steps
1. Cut the narration for the picked sentences and join them with a short pause.
2. Stills of every reshot section, check the framing.
3. Render JPG sequences, assemble, add captions and cards in a vertical layout.
4. Watch on a phone.

## Output
A 60-90 s 1080x1920 video with captions, the highlight list, and the untouched 16:9 originals.
